# Ethical Principles and Professional Responsibility – Answers

> Note: All answers are grounded in the repository’s core statement about accountability, transparency, stakeholders, and ethical decision-making [1].

## 1) Are ethical principles and professional responsibility the same?
No. Ethical principles are the underlying moral values (such as accountability and transparency), while professional responsibility is the duty to apply those values in practice when making decisions and serving stakeholders [1].

## 2) What type of ethical issues will you find in your field? Name several and justify your rationale.
In computational sciences, common ethical issues include:
- **Lack of transparency** in system behavior and decision-making.
- **Weak accountability** when harms occur and no one takes responsibility.
- **Stakeholder harm** when decisions ignore the people affected by technical systems.

These are justified because the repository’s README emphasizes that professionals must be accountable and transparent to all stakeholders, and that decisions must always consider ethical and professional principles [1].

## 3) Analyze those issues from a human value and dignity standpoint. How do you apply them from a Christian worldview? How do technical choices impact human lives?
From a human dignity standpoint, transparency and accountability protect people from being treated as mere data points and instead recognize them as persons who deserve fairness, respect, and explanation.  
From a Christian worldview, ethical decisions should reflect love of neighbor, truthfulness, justice, and care for vulnerable people; this aligns with making decisions that are transparent, accountable, and stakeholder-centered.  
Technical choices affect real lives by shaping access to opportunities, privacy, safety, trust, and social outcomes; when ethics are ignored, harm can spread quickly through systems that affect many stakeholders [1].

## 4) Where does a professional learn about ethical norms within their field?
A professional learns ethical norms through formal education, professional standards, organizational policies, mentorship, and ongoing practice that reinforces accountability and transparency in stakeholder-facing decisions [1].

## 5) Why is it important to adhere to ethical norms in research?
Adhering to ethical norms in research is essential to protect participants and affected communities, maintain trust, ensure integrity, and keep decisions aligned with accountability and transparency obligations to stakeholders [1].
